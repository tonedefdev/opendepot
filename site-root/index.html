Continuously reconciled IaC

Infrastructure, on your terms.

OpenDepot is a self-hosted, Kubernetes-native registry where Depots continuously reconcile OpenTofu and Terraform modules and providers. Define distribution in manifests and let OpenDepot keep your registry in sync.

The Depot control loop

From source change to ready-to-use infrastructure.

Depots pull from the sources you trust, reconcile what changed, and publish a verified registry that OpenTofu and Terraform can consume.
