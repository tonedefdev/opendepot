Fine-grained scan policy

Waive one finding. Keep every other gate.

Global thresholds block everything or nothing. ScanPolicy sets severity and exact, expiring exemptions per namespace, module, provider, or version range, and the Registry Explorer lets the right people manage them.

  1. 01
    Target precisely

    Pin a policy to named Modules or Providers and a version constraint, or cover a group with a label selector.

  2. 02
    Highest priority wins

    Policies never merge. The winner applies in full, and every other match reports who superseded it.

  3. 03
    Exemptions expire

    Exempted findings stay visible on the Version. When an exemption expires, blocking resumes on its own.

  4. 04
    Scoped administration

    SecurityGroupBindings map OIDC groups to namespaces and resources. The server re-authorizes every preview, save, and delete, and fails closed.

Assembly Line

Compose root modules from parts you already trust.

Wire module outputs to inputs on a visual canvas built from your onboarded, scanned versions. OpenDepot validates the result against your own registry and hands back clean OpenTofu.

  1. 01Select

    Modules and OpenTofu providers pinned to exact versions with extracted contracts.

  2. 02Wire

    Connect variables and outputs to inputs, alias providers, and repeat modules with count or for_each.

  3. 03Validate

    The server re-authorizes every node and runs tofu init through OpenDepot's provider mirror. No public fallback.

  4. 04Export

    Download main.tf and variables.tf. No state, lock files, or credentials.
