Continuously reconciled IaC

Infrastructure, on your terms.

OpenDepot is a self-hosted, Kubernetes-native registry where Depots continuously reconcile OpenTofu and Terraform modules and providers, keep them current, and scan every asset with Trivy. Define distribution in manifests, review changes in Git, and let Argo CD or Flux deploy them so OpenDepot can keep your registry in sync.
